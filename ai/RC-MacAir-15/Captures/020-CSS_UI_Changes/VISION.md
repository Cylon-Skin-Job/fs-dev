# VISION — Component System And Wiki Renewal

> Long-range owner direction for rebuilding Fusion Studio's component language and bringing the Wiki into alignment. This document records direction, not implementation approval.

## Component Direction

- Craft the component system deliberately, one component and interaction pattern at a time.
- Develop a coherent shared language without forcing decisions before the behavior is clear.
- Preserve hazy or unresolved areas as open parts of the vision rather than filling them with assumptions.

## Wiki Direction

- Start at the top of the Wiki and work downward again.
- Reconsider each affected article from a fresh reading instead of treating earlier audits as final authority.
- Expect some existing material to be obsolete and some to be substantially stale.
- Use current code, active refactors, and the owner's evolving component direction to decide what remains durable.

## Current Transition

- The first component refactor is approximately halfway complete.
- Documentation must distinguish the current implementation, the in-progress refactor, and the intended end state.
- Do not prematurely rewrite the Wiki around incomplete implementation details that may still change.

## Open Vision

- The detailed sequence for crafting components will be developed with the owner step by step.
- Unsettled component boundaries and interaction rules remain open until the owner works through them.
