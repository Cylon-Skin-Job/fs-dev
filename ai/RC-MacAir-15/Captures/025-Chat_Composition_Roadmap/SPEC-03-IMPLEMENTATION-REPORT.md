## Delivered outcome (whole SPEC)

Fusion Studio's view-bound Thread Groups now remember the content worksurface that belongs to them:

- **Canonical ownership and key.** The view-state document remains the sole owner of worksurface content under `viewStates[viewId].threadWorksurfaces[threadGroupId]`, addressed by `{workspaceId, viewId, threadGroupId}`. `threadId` remains the transcript/routing/Provenance identity and is never the worksurface key; no worksurface lane, request, result, or fan-out carries `surfaceId`; Legacy (`viewId: null`) never has an entry.
- **One versioned adapter contract.** `capture`/`sanitize`/`restore` with strict size/type/version validation and inert unavailable-content handling. Adapters are explicitly registered in `builtins.ts`.
- **Participating built-in views (03D).** `file-viewer` (03A, `activity` tabs/active/recents/navigation), `wiki-viewer` (03B, `activity.navigation`), and the 03D remaining in-scope views: `capture-viewer` (classic document mode/selections/scrolls, classic tab collection/active tab, activity recents/navigation), `office-viewer` (mode, current folder, selected document, document side panel, activity recents), and `email-viewer` (mode, current folder, selected document, document side panel, activity recents). Each participating view mounts the collapsed `ViewWorksurfaceDock` as its production group-selection path.
- **Cutover.** While a view is group-bound, its adapter/controller is the sole writer/hydrator of every elected content fact. The legacy global `state:set` write-through for those facts (`activity`, classic Capture document navigation and tabs, Office document navigation) is skipped and routed into the selected group's content lane; the non-group/Legacy path keeps the exact existing persistence and cannot hydrate over a group entry.
- **Switching lifecycle.** Acknowledgement-gated switch (freeze → CAS submit → keep outgoing selected → correlated success → bind/open incoming → exact GET → sanitize/restore), flush-before-leave for view switch/panel close/detach/teardown, non-destructive conflict retention with warned Retry/Switch-without-saving, late-ack/inactive-mount isolation, clean/dirty multi-window fan-out, and reconnect re-hydration.
- **Durable delete cleanup (03C).** Group deletion atomically records a `remove-group-worksurface` outbox row; the view-state service consumes it idempotently and removes only the exact entry, with retry/observability/restart recovery. Chat/Provenance are never cascaded.
- **Controller size hygiene (03D).** The 03B-D8 commitment is resolved: the 895-line `worksurfaceController.ts` is a stable public facade over five focused modules, all far below the routed 400-line checklist, with the public import surface unchanged.
