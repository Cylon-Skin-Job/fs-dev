Thus B12's persistent visibilityState=visible likewise cannot establish actual foreground. The Playwright Electron loader also appends Chromium switches including disable-background-timer-throttling, disable-backgrounding-occluded-windows, disable-renderer-backgrounding and enable-automation. Those flags change automation/background policy; no inspected source establishes them as the actor that generated B12's native hide/blur. Focus emulation is a protocol command, not merely a command-line flag.
