**Contacts** is present as a bundled `contacts-viewer` template. Its manifest declares `enabled: false` and `availability: experimental`, so it is a cataloged idea rather than a default installed view. The current five-view New Workspace profile does not select it.

## Current status and limits

The fixed `ContentArea` built-in component map has no Contacts renderer. A configured panel could follow the generic local-app or iframe branches if it supplied their required configuration, or otherwise reach the generic placeholder; template existence does not demonstrate a working contacts application or a connected address book. The manifest's SQLite data-source label is not evidence of a live contacts store. The future plugin model may define a Contacts view, but installation, external source authority and permissions have not been specified here.

See [View Architecture](../002-View_Architecture/PAGE.md) for current dispatch and future plugin identity. The [View Catalog](../023-View_Catalog/PAGE.md) compares current availability across the bundled templates.
