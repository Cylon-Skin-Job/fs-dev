**Media** is a bundled `media-viewer` template describing a user-selected folder in the workspace repository. The template can appear in the catalog without being selected by the current five-view New Workspace profile. Its `selected-folder` declaration does not identify a concrete folder or guarantee media indexing and playback.

## Current status and limits

There is no Media component in the current fixed `ContentArea` built-in map. A configured panel with a local app or iframe could take one of the generic rendering branches; without that it reaches the placeholder. This is not a verified Media application. The future plugin design could supply display and content behavior while an editable instance chooses its content root within allowed capabilities. Exact binding and permission schemas are still open.

Read [View Architecture](../002-View_Architecture/PAGE.md) for current dispatch versus the intended plugin model, and [View Catalog](../023-View_Catalog/PAGE.md) for the full status list.
