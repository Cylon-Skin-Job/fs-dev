Bite-size vision for the Fusion Home domain. Details live in the view and
editor-surface articles.

## What Fusion Home Shows

Fusion Home is a curated profile and a place to explain the Office surface and related document work. The current `fusion-home` profile selects Office, Files, Issues, Wiki and Agents. Calendar, Email and ToDo remain separate subjects whose implemented status must be read from their current view owners. The longer office-app composition is a vision, not evidence that all those apps are mounted together today.

The [Office Viewer](../../001-Office_Viewer/PAGE.md) and [Documents](../../005-Documents/PAGE.md) articles own current editing behavior. [System Manager](../../../001-Workspaces_And_Views/011-System_Manager/PAGE.md) owns the separate System surface. A connected app or service remains authoritative for its live content; a future composition should use the [Workspace Compositions](../../../001-Workspaces_And_Views/021-Workspace_Compositions/PAGE.md) boundary rather than treating the System database as a shared app-content store.

## Progressive Disclosure

The structure mirrors progressive disclosure: the Fusion Home heading gives the
model (views vs. editor surfaces); each app article gives the user-facing
behavior; each sub-article (e.g. [Tables](../../005-Documents/004-Tables/PAGE.md))
goes technical. A reader can stop at the depth they need.

## Where It's Going

- Develop Calendar and Email beyond their current demo/mock interfaces; define ToDo, Sheets, PDFs and Artifacts separately. Their presence in this vision does not establish completed integrations.
- Keep the Office Viewer's own sub-articles (layout, search, starred/pinned) as
  the canonical home for view-level behavior.
- Treat this Vision page as the durable "why," not the "how."
