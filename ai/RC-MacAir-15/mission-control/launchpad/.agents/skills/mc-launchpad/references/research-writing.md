# Research and Writing Work Profile

Load this profile for reports, cited articles, academic assignments, literature reviews, evidence-backed essays, policy analysis, or research that will shape another deliverable.

## Typical Evidence

- Preserve bibliographic identity, stable links or identifiers, publication dates, and retrieval context.
- Separate exact quotation, paraphrase, source claim, and agent inference.
- Record study or source limitations rather than flattening disagreement into one conclusion.
- Prefer primary evidence for claims about research results and authoritative sources for rules or standards.
- Match citation rigor to the stakes and intended audience.

## Candidate Extension Documents

| Document | Kind | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| `CORPUS.md` | `evidence_corpus` | Multiple sources need durable metadata, relevance notes, quotations, or extraction status. |
| `COMPARISONS.md` | `source_comparison` | Sources must be compared across common questions, methods, populations, or conclusions. |
| `CLAIM_MAP.md` | `claim_map` | Important draft claims need explicit supporting, conflicting, or missing evidence. |
| `BLOG_MAP.md` or `PRIOR_WORK.md` | `prior_work_map` | Earlier writing or reusable work must be discoverable by topic and relationship. |
| `OUTLINE.md` | `deliverable_structure` | The argument or report structure needs to evolve separately from the evidence corpus. |
| `DRAFT.md` | `working_draft` | Continuous prose has begun and needs a lifecycle distinct from notes and evidence. |
| `SOURCE_AUDIT.md` | `source_audit` | High-assurance work requires explicit checks for citation accuracy, corrections, retractions, or unsupported claims. |

Do not turn a bibliography into a corpus unless annotations, comparison, extraction, or reuse justify the additional structure.

## High-Stakes Domain Overlay

For medical, legal, financial, safety, or similarly consequential work, consider fields for source type, jurisdiction or population, study design, sample size, publication status, DOI/PMID or equivalent identifier, exact quote location, limitations, conflicts, corrections or retractions, and date checked. Require direct support for consequential claims and label uncertainty prominently.

## Useful Procedures

- Source discovery and screening
- Quote and citation extraction
- Evidence-table construction
- Multi-source comparison
- Claim-to-source mapping
- Outline synthesis
- Drafting with provenance
- Citation and inference audit

## Handoff Signals

Writing can begin before research is exhaustive, but the project approaches stable production readiness when central claims have traceable support, material disagreements and limitations are visible, the intended audience and form are explicit, and the outline separates sourced conclusions from open questions.
