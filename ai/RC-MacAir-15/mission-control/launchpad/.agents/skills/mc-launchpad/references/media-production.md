# Media Production Work Profile

Load this profile for video, audio, tutorials, demonstrations, courses, scripts, storyboards, or other time-based production.

## Typical Evidence and Inputs

- Distinguish factual sources from creative references and aesthetic inspiration.
- Track rights, attribution, licensing, and availability for external assets when relevant.
- Separate what must be said, what must be shown, and what must be produced.
- Treat demonstrations and screenshots as claims about current behavior that may need fresh verification.

## Candidate Extension Documents

| Document | Kind | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| `SOURCES.md` | `evidence_corpus` | Factual sources, visual references, assets, or attribution obligations need durable tracking. |
| `VIDEO_MAP.md` or `SERIES_MAP.md` | `prior_work_map` | Existing or planned media must be navigated as a collection. |
| `OUTLINE.md` | `deliverable_structure` | Topic order and teaching or narrative beats need shaping before full scripting. |
| `SCRIPT.md` | `working_draft` | Spoken words, narration, dialogue, or on-screen text have become a maintained draft. |
| `STORYBOARD.md` | `storyboard` | Visual beats, framing, or scene transitions need independent iteration. |
| `SHOT_LIST.md` | `production_map` | Concrete capture requirements, locations, assets, performers, or states must be scheduled. |
| `DEMO_MAP.md` | `demonstration_plan` | Software states, sample data, reset steps, or failure contingencies need reproducible planning. |
| `ASSET_REGISTER.md` | `asset_register` | Reusable media, rights, provenance, and production status form a substantial collection. |

Do not create a storyboard for an audio-only piece or a shot list for a simple narrated screen recording unless the production complexity earns it.

## Useful Procedures

- Audience and learning-outcome framing
- Research and factual verification
- Narrative or instructional outlining
- Script drafting
- Storyboarding
- Demonstration rehearsal
- Shot and asset planning
- Fact, continuity, and rights review

## Handoff Signals

Production approaches readiness when the audience and desired effect are explicit, factual claims are supportable, the outline or script is coherent, every required visual or demonstration has an attainable source, and unresolved creative choices are visible rather than hidden in production notes.
