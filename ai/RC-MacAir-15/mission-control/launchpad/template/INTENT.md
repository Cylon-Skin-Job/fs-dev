# INTENT — Owner direction template

> Inert starter: adapt to the assigned subject. Owner-directed purpose, outcomes and boundaries. Intent establishes direction, not implementation authorization. Prompts are not evidence of settled intent.

Attribute substantive statements to explicit owner direction or linked decisions. Leave unsupported points visibly unresolved and link the relevant Capture question or issue; never fill a section by guessing. An unknown, an intentional deferral and an explicit non-goal are different things.

## Purpose

Why does this body of work exist? What problem or opportunity prompted the conversation, and for whom?

## Desired Outcomes

What should become true in this effort? Describe observable results before choosing mechanisms. Keep candidate methods in PROPOSALS and implementation work in later SPECs.

## Enduring Goals

What longer-term direction should this work contribute toward? Keep this separate from current scope. Omit this optional section and its index entry when no substantive enduring goal exists.

## Success Conditions

What evidence would let the owner recognize that the desired outcomes have been achieved? At this stage, plain-language scenarios are enough; surface an unclear success condition rather than inventing detailed tests.

## Constraints

What established product, architecture, compatibility, standards, resource or workflow boundaries must be respected? Link authoritative sources and distinguish required constraints from preferences or unverified assumptions.

## Non-goals

What has explicitly been excluded from this effort? Do not infer an exclusion merely because it has not been discussed. Record future interests as such rather than expanding current scope.
