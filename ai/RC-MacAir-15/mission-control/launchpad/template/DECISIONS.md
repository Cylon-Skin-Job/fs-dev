# DECISIONS — Owner choices template

> Inert starter: adapt to the assigned subject. Records explicit owner choices and their scope. Research findings and assistant recommendations cannot create owner approval. No decisions exist in this template.

Group records under stable subject categories, adapting the starting categories below. Use neutral `D-` IDs and bold-label bullet fields: **Date**, **Category**, **Status**, **Source**, **Decision**, **Rationale**, **Implications**, **Supersedes**, and **Related**. Status is active, superseded, or revoked. Use “none” where appropriate; if rationale was not stated, say so instead of inventing it.

For a clear change of mind, preserve the earlier decision and link the newer one, identifying exactly which scope changed. Do not silently rewrite history or ask for redundant confirmation. If the later statement could concern a different scope, be tentative, or admit conflicting interpretations, raise an issue and link it before claiming supersession. Propagate a settled change to affected intent, proposals and draft sections; keep unresolved propagation visible.
