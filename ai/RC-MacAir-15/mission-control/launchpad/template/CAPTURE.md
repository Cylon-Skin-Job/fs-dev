# CAPTURE — Working memory template

> Inert starter: adapt to the assigned subject. Curated conversation memory and open loops, not a transcript or approval record. Prompts below guide the curator; they are not project findings. Replace them with sourced content when used.

Use stable `CAP-` IDs for individual threads under the relevant section. Each record carries **Origin**, **Type**, **Status**, **Source**, **Summary**, and **Related** as bold-label bullet fields. Active threads also carry **Next**. Origin distinguishes user, assistant, or mixed; status is open, parked, routed, or closed. Cite the conversation/turn when available; otherwise give honest attribution and coverage limits. Never invent history identifiers.

## Working Synthesis

What are we discussing, why does it matter, and where did we leave off? Summarize settled direction, tentative ideas, material changes since the previous synthesis, and a useful next conversational step. Link the controlling intent, decisions and issues. Say which conversation interval or records support this synopsis; do not imply an exhaustive reread.

## User Threads to Resume

What did the owner mention that a tangent displaced? Preserve the original idea and context, where discussion stopped, and a natural re-entry question. Distinguish the owner's words from the assistant's interpretation. An unexplored topic is not automatically a problem or an approved requirement.

## Assistant Possibilities

What related possibilities did the assistant introduce that the owner has not endorsed? Keep that origin visible. Move a developed candidate action into PROPOSALS when useful; do not let repetition turn it into owner intent.

## Decision Queue

Which concrete choices need the owner, and why now? Give the affected outcome, options and implications when grounded. Link consequential ambiguity to ISSUES rather than copying the issue. Keep questions answerable and avoid asking the owner to repeat an already explicit choice.

## Routed Outcomes

When a thread matures, preserve its ID and source, link its authoritative destination, and retain a short outcome. A routed record uses **Status: routed** and an **Outcome** field. A closed thread states its closure reason in **Outcome**. Promotion transfers record responsibility; it does not prove implementation or acceptance. Use the shared routing procedure instead of maintaining duplicate versions.

## Capture History

Keep brief dated notes about meaningful synthesis checkpoints: author, source coverage, changed understanding and destination links. Use dated bullets, not new CAP records, for these notes. A reminder or a targeted history review does not itself advance CHECKPOINT.json.
